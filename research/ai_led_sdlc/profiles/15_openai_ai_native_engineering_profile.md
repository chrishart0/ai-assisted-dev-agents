
# OpenAI AI-Native Engineering Profile

## What it is
OpenAI’s AI-native engineering guidance is an operating model for how engineering teams can distribute work between humans and coding agents across planning, design, build, test, review, documentation, and maintenance. [S11]

## Workflow shape
The guidance is not a single prescribed ceremony stack. Instead, it breaks the lifecycle into planning, design, build, test, review, document, and deploy/maintain, supported by durable context and workflow artifacts such as `AGENTS.md` and `PLAN.md`. [S11]

## Distinctive artifacts
- `AGENTS.md`
- `PLAN.md`
- prompt/spec files for goals and non-goals
- implementation instructions
- documentation/status files that act as shared memory and audit logs [S11]

## How AI participates
OpenAI describes agents contributing to planning, design, development, testing, code review, and deployment support. The long-horizon guidance is especially strong on turning open-ended work into milestones, validation steps, and shared memory. [S11]

## How humans stay in control
The operating principle is “delegate, review, own.” Humans delegate first-pass work but remain responsible for architecture, correctness, production decisions, and accountability. [S11]

## Why it matters
OpenAI contributes a strong human/agent operating philosophy and practical artifact design. It is one of the best sources in the corpus for lightweight but durable context and long-running task structure. [S11]

## Best fit
Teams that want a portable operating model and durable repo conventions without adopting a heavy branded method.
