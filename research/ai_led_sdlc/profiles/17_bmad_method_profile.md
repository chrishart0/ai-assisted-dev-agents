
# BMAD Method Profile

## What it is
BMAD is a public method focused on context engineering for AI-powered development. It makes the front half of the lifecycle more explicit through analysis, planning, solutioning, and implementation. [S9]

## Workflow shape
The core workflow is:
1. analysis;
2. planning;
3. solutioning;
4. implementation. [S9]

BMAD also supports different depth tracks depending on project complexity.

## Distinctive artifacts
- product brief
- PRD
- UX spec (when needed)
- architecture
- epics and stories
- `project-context.md` [S9]

## How AI participates
BMAD assumes AI agents will perform much of the drafting and implementation work, but only after a progressively richer context foundation is built. [S9]

## How humans stay in control
Humans are expected to guide the planning and solutioning phases, validate context artifacts, and perform review. BMAD also emphasizes project-context persistence to keep future agent work aligned. [S9]

## Why it matters
BMAD’s biggest contribution is exposing the hidden middle between problem statement and implementation. Its “solutioning” emphasis is especially valuable in larger or multi-epic work where agents can otherwise make inconsistent technical decisions. [S9]

## Best fit
Teams that want an adaptive, practical planning and solutioning method they can pair with an enterprise platform and governance layer.
